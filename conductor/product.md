# Product Guide: patterns-sa

## Initial Concept
A toolkit to perform sensitivity analysis and explainability of architectural tradeoffs on patterns.

## Target Audience
The toolkit is primarily designed for **Software Architects and Researchers**. It provides the rigorous analytical tools necessary for deep architectural investigation and academic research into how design patterns behave under varying conditions.

## Problem Statement
Architectural decision-making often lacks quantitative grounding. `patterns-sa` addresses the following challenges:
- **Lack of Quantitative Tools:** Bridging the gap between qualitative pattern descriptions and quantitative assessment of quality attributes.
- **Complex Trade-off Spaces:** Providing methods to visualize and comprehend the high-dimensional trade-offs inherent in design decisions.
- **Fragmented Analysis:** Offering a unified, extensible framework to compare architectural strategies across diverse domains like Microservices and Federated Learning.

## Key Features
- **Quantitative Trade-off Analysis:** Rigorous assessment of architectural sensitivity and robustness using data-driven metrics.
- **Explainable Design Decisions:** Leveraging scenario discovery techniques (like PRIM and CART) to explain *why* certain architectural choices succeed or fail.
- **Extensible Architecture:** A plugin-based system that allows for easy integration of new patterns, domains, and simulation data formats.

## Vision
To serve as a premier **open-source research platform** for exploring and advancing new analysis techniques in the field of software architecture, fostering a community-driven approach to architectural evaluation.
