# Image task synthesis — RQ1 and RQ2

This refresh covers the 13 executed image notebooks in the [evidence manifest](evidence-manifest.json), scoped by [NOTEBOOK_STRATIFICATION.md](../NOTEBOOK_STRATIFICATION.md). The leaves overlap: fixed-client all-configuration views, isolated stress views, common-stress combined-configuration views, and single-configuration profiles answer distinct questions. Do not sum their counts or call them independent replications.

## RQ1 — Which tradeoffs occur, and how do design choices relate to them?

Metric semantics set preference before occupancy: maximize `final_val_accuracy`, minimize `avg_total_time`; the ideal is `L-S`. The fixed bins span accuracy 0.2075–1.0172 and total time 1.5625–25.3945. Case distributions concentrate in middle accuracy regions with short/medium time, while the preferred high-accuracy/low-time corner is generally empty in these views. That means it was not observed in these samples, not that it is impossible. Use each case's separate train/test region counts because held-out allocation can differ.

The c4/c8/c10 leaves retain all eight configurations and support within-client-stratum comparisons. Stress-only leaves isolate one active pattern while pooling clients. Combined-stress leaves compare supported complete configurations at common stress: seven at 25%, six at 50% and 75%; the triple-pattern configuration is unavailable in the latter two. Profile leaves describe one policy across pooled clients and do not directly compare it with another policy. Context varies in pooled leaves, so associations combine configuration and sampled environment/client conditions. Marginal pattern counts are not complete configurations.

## RQ2 — Which joint conditions predict target tradeoffs, and what guidance do they support?

PRIM/CART rules are learned on grouped training seeds and evaluated on held-out seeds. Conditions and feature rankings vary across scopes. Client count, policy/stress, and environment or data heterogeneity can appear jointly, and ranks shift when a slice fixes or aliases parameters. Treat each rule as a scope-bound operating envelope, not an independent effect. The held-out tables give target prevalence, box precision, absolute lift, target coverage and retained support; case reports include these numbers and policy-specific matrices. Small or absent held-out targets make validation weak or unavailable. Section 11 policy matrices describe outcomes within a policy group after filtering and must be interpreted with target coverage/retained counts; they are distinct from baseline robustness.

**Practitioner implication:** use boxes to prioritize controlled image-task trials, preserving full configuration and operating context. Confirm on additional seeds before design commitment.

**Researcher implication:** fill the preferred region with adequately supported observations and separate pattern decisions from environment/client composition in controlled comparisons. Report grouped split support and full region distributions on both partitions.

## Limits

Conclusions apply to CIFAR-10/CNN 16k and the sampled compact campaign. Notebook views overlap and have unequal support; compact client measures are run-level aggregates. Scenario discovery is predictive and observational and does not prove causality or guarantee behavior outside the tested slice. The reports cite notebook outputs, box reports and plots; notebooks were not rerun.
